Aurum

Mediterranean cuisine, served the way it deserves.

A table above the Carthage coast

Aurum opened in 2014 in a restored 1930s villa. Chef Karim Belhaj cooks with fish landed that morning, olive oil from Sfax and vegetables from a family farm in Cap Bon. The dining room seats forty, so every guest is looked after by name.

Season firstThe menu changes with the market, never with the calendar.
Unhurried serviceDinner lasts as long as you want it to.
A deep cellarThree hundred labels, with a focus on Tunisian and Mediterranean growers.
Candlelight, linen and brass.
